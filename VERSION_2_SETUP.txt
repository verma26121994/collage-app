College Lesson Hub – Version 2

This build reads published lessons from the teacher's published Google Sheets CSV.
Only rows whose Status is exactly Published are displayed.

Sheet columns supported:
Subject | Unit | Lecture | Description | PDF Link | Status

PDF Link should contain a URL to a viewable PDF. Lecture 1 also has a local fallback PDF.

The app keeps the same student URL. Students do not need to reinstall for new rows.
